Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

1. Data We Collect

Account data: username, email, hashed password, optional avatar & bio.
Content data: chat messages, uploaded documents, memories, and images you attach.
Usage data: login IP/user-agent (security audit), API request logs (latency, status), and anonymous error logs.

2. How We Use Your Data

To operate the service: generating AI responses, searching documents you upload, personalizing memory, and securing the platform (rate limiting, ban enforcement). We never sell your data.

3. AI Providers

Messages may be sent to free inference providers (Groq, Google Gemini, Pollinations) strictly to generate your response. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information in chats.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use a session cookie for login, and localStorage for theme, voice and consent preferences. Optional analytics load only after you accept the cookie banner.

5. Storage & Retention

Data is stored in an encrypted-at-rest PostgreSQL database (Neon). You may delete chats, documents, memories, or your entire account at any time.

6. Your Rights

Access, export, correction and deletion of your data. Contact us below to exercise any right.
