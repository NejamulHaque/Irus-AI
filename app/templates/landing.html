Frequently Asked Questions

Is Irus AI really free?

Yes — 100% free. It runs on free-tier AI providers (Groq, Gemini, Pollinations) and free cloud hosting.

What can Irus AI do?

Streaming chat, live web search with citations, PDF/Word document Q&A, image analysis, AI art generation, voice input/output and persistent memory.

Is my data private?

Your data is stored securely, never sold, and you can delete anything at any time. Read the Privacy Policy.

Can I use Irus AI in my own apps?

Yes! Create an API key in the Developer Console and call POST /api/v1/chat from any language.

Does it work on mobile?

Fully responsive, and installable as a PWA — add it to your home screen for a native app experience.
