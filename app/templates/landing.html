Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about models, security, and usage.

Is Irus AI really 100% free?

Yes! Irus AI is completely free to use. It leverages high-performance free tiers on Groq LPU inference, Google Gemini vision models, and Pollinations generative AI, running on cloud infrastructure engineered by Nejamul Haque.

How is Irus AI so much faster than other AI chats?

Irus AI runs primary LLM inference on Groq's custom Language Processing Units (LPUs). LPUs utilize massive on-chip SRAM bandwidth rather than traditional GPU memory architectures, delivering up to 820+ tokens per second.

Is my uploaded data and conversation history private?

Yes. Your documents and chats are stored strictly within your account. We never sell your personal data or use your private conversations to train public AI models. You can purge or export your data at any time.

Can I use Irus AI in my own code via API?

Yes! Visit your Profile & Developer tab after signing in to generate your API keys. You can call POST /api/v1/chat from Python, JavaScript, cURL, or any HTTP client.

Does Irus AI work on mobile as an app?

Yes. Irus AI is a full Progressive Web App (PWA). You can tap "Install App" or "Add to Home Screen" on iOS and Android to get full-screen native performance.
